# 📌 Recommendations

Based on the Cyclistic ride data analysis, here are the top three actionable recommendations to increase annual memberships and revenue stability.

1️⃣ **Weekend-to-Weekday Incentives**
Offer special discounts or reward points to casual riders who ride mainly on weekends, encouraging them to ride more frequently on weekdays. This bridges the usage pattern of casuals to match that of members.

2️⃣ **Membership Bundles with Flexible Benefits**
Launch a seasonal membership bundle (e.g., 3-month or 6-month) with clear cost savings for frequent weekend riders. Highlight unlimited rides, lower cost per ride, and extra perks like free ride credits or partner discounts.

3️⃣ **Personalized Marketing Campaigns**
Use historical trip data to segment casual riders by ride duration and frequency. Send targeted emails and in-app promotions showing exactly how much they could save annually if they switch to a membership.
